## Boilerplate (short)
Pulse Calendar is a calendar for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch that helps you get things done. It puts the weather inside every day, reminds you of what comes back, starts a focus timer in one tap, and answers questions about your schedule with Apple's on-device AI. No account, no tracking: your events stay on your devices.

## Boilerplate (long)
Pulse Calendar is built by a solo developer in France around one idea: a calendar should help you act, not just tell you where to be. It reads your existing iCloud, Google and Outlook calendars, puts each day's weather right inside your views (WeatherKit), notices the things that come back, like the dentist, a haircut or a car service, and reminds you when it's time. When you keep putting something off, Start now launches a timer in one tap, with a Live Activity on the Lock Screen. Ask Pulse answers questions about your schedule with Apple's Foundation Models, so your questions never leave your iPhone. Version 2.3.4 is ready for iPhone Duo on day one: folded, the essentials; unfolded, your whole week on one screen. Native on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, in 14 languages.

## Key features
- **Weather in your calendar (WeatherKit):** each day's temperature and conditions, plus a nudge to move outdoor plans away from the rain.
- **What comes back:** Pulse spots recurring things and reminds you when it's time.
- **Start now:** a one-tap focus timer, with a Live Activity on the Lock Screen.
- **Ask Pulse:** talk to your calendar in plain words, on device.
- **Ready for iPhone Duo:** folded, the essentials; unfolded, your whole week.
- **Smarter notifications:** one reminder per appointment, a heads-up when it's time to leave, a morning briefing and an evening preview.
- **iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch:** native apps for every screen.
- **Private by design:** events stay on the device, synced through the user's own iCloud. No tracking, no ads.
