About

Pulse Calendar is a calendar that helps you get things done. It puts the weather inside every day, notices what comes back (the dentist, a haircut, a car service) and reminds you when it's time, and starts a focus timer in one tap when you keep putting something off. Ask Pulse answers questions about your schedule with Apple's on-device AI: no account, and your events stay on your iPhone.

Version 2.3.4 is ready for iPhone Duo on day one: folded, the essentials; unfolded, your whole week on one screen. Pulse is native on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, available in 14 languages, and built on Apple frameworks end to end: Foundation Models, WeatherKit, HealthKit, WidgetKit & Live Activities, App Intents, SwiftUI, Mac Catalyst and StoreKit 2.

Weather in your calendar
